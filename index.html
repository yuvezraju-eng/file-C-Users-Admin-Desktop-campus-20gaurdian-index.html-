Campus Guardian allows students and staff to report campus problems, track their progress and help administrators resolve them efficiently.

How Campus Guardian Works

From reporting to resolution.

📝

1. Report

Students and teachers can report broken equipment, cleanliness problems, water leakage, infrastructure issues and more.

🔍

2. Track

Administrators can view, prioritize and assign reported problems to maintenance teams.

🔧

3. Resolve

Maintenance teams can update the status until the problem is completely resolved.
